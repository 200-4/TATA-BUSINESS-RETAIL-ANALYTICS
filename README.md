# TATA-BUSINESS-RETAIL-ANALYTICS
TATA Group is a global company which operates in more than 100 countries across six continents, with a mission to 'improve the quality of life of the communities we serve'
This project focused on a Job Simulation that was organised by one of the TATA companies known as the TATA INsights and Quants or TATA IQ
## OBJECTIVES
The major objective is to answer questions cited by the CMO and CEO
To Acquire or gain insights from the data using data visualization solutions
To create amazing stories which are hidden under mountains of data generated
## SKILLS
Data Visualization
Data Analysis
Data Interpretation
Visual Basic

## KPI's
### CEO
#### Interested in viewing Time series Data for the Year 2011 only
#### He's also interested in viewing the demand of their products from different regions excluding United Kingdom
### CMO
#### The CMO is interested in viewing the top 10 countries which generated the highest revenue,additionally also gain insight on the quantity sold along the revenue generated
#### The CMO is also interested in viewing greatest generating customers
